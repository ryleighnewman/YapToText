## Description

YapToText is a free, open-source speech to text app for the Mac. Press one key, talk, and
your words are typed wherever your cursor is, in any app. The speech model and the cleanup
model both run on your Mac, so nothing you say leaves it. No account, no subscription, no
word cap.

What you see in the video:
